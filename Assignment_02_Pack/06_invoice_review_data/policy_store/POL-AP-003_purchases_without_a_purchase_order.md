# Purchases Without a Purchase Order

## Position

The organisation operates a no-PO-no-pay position. An invoice arriving without a valid
purchase order reference is, by default, an exception.

## Permitted exceptions

Certain categories may be procured without a purchase order, in recognition that the
commitment is made at the point of service rather than in advance:

- **Professional Services** engaged on an ad-hoc or call-out basis, where the total
  invoice value does not exceed one thousand dollars (AUD 1,000);
- utilities, rates and statutory charges;
- travel booked through the approved travel provider.

Purchases in any other category, including **Raw Materials**, **Consumables** and
**Capital Equipment**, require a purchase order in all circumstances regardless of value.

## Treatment

Where a non-PO invoice falls within a permitted category and threshold, it may be
processed on the approval of the cost centre owner. Where it does not, the invoice is
held and referred to Procurement, who will determine whether the purchase is to be
retrospectively authorised or rejected.

Repeated non-PO purchasing from the same supplier should be reported to the category
manager irrespective of whether individual invoices were permitted.
