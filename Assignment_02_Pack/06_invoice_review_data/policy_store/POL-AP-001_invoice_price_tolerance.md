# Invoice Price Tolerance

## Purpose

To permit efficient processing of supplier invoices where the invoiced price differs
immaterially from the price agreed on the purchase order, without requiring manual
intervention for every minor variance.

## Standard tolerance

Where the invoiced unit price exceeds the purchase order unit price, Accounts Payable
may accept the invoice without escalation provided the variance does **not exceed five
per cent (5%)** of the purchase order unit price.

Variances of any size in the supplier's favour to the buyer — that is, an invoiced price
*below* the purchase order price — are accepted without escalation.

## Where the standard tolerance does not apply

The tolerance in this policy is the organisation's default position. It applies **except
where a more specific commercial arrangement is in force with the supplier**, in which
case the terms of that arrangement prevail. See POL-AP-002.

The standard tolerance does not apply to:

- capital equipment purchases, which are governed by the capital approval framework;
- suppliers whose account is on hold;
- suppliers whose commercial agreement has lapsed.

## Cumulative variance

Where a single invoice contains multiple lines, tolerance is assessed **per line**.
An invoice may therefore be partially within tolerance. Where the aggregate variance
across the invoice exceeds five per cent of the invoice total, the invoice should be
escalated in full even where individual lines are within tolerance.
