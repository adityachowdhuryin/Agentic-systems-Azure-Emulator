# Negotiated Commercial Agreements — Price Discipline

## Purpose

Suppliers who have entered a negotiated commercial agreement with the organisation
receive preferential volume commitments and extended payment terms. In return, they
accept tighter price discipline than the organisation's standard position.

## Tolerance under a negotiated agreement

Where a supplier holds a current negotiated commercial agreement, the price tolerance
recorded against that supplier in the vendor master applies to all invoices raised under
that agreement.

**This tolerance is generally tighter than the organisation's standard tolerance and
replaces it.** Where the two differ, the negotiated figure governs.

## Validity

A negotiated agreement applies only while it is current. Where the agreement period has
lapsed and has not been renewed, invoices from that supplier are handled under the
standard arrangements in POL-AP-001 — but Accounts Payable should note the lapse, as
continued trading on expired terms is a procurement exception in its own right.

## Escalation

Invoices exceeding the negotiated tolerance are escalated to the category manager rather
than to Accounts Payable supervision, as the appropriate remedy is usually commercial
rather than clerical.
